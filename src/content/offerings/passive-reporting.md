The service is **yours**. You keep root, you keep full control of the
environment, and you decide how it evolves. ConcertIM stays on the outside:
we watch your environment continuously and turn what we see into a
**health report** on a schedule you choose.

A report isn't a raw dump — it's a short list of **observations**. Each one
says what we found, what it could mean for your service if ignored, and
what we recommend doing about it. How you address them is up to you: fix
them yourself, or hand the observations back and we'll take it from there.

We also walk the environment the way your users do, so the report covers
**feel** — is anything running slow? has scheduler capacity changed? — not
just hardware and disk.

**Best for** teams that can run their own platform and want scheduled
health insight, with backup when it's needed.
