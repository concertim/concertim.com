Here we compile our notes from experience delivering managed services and
looking after a variety of HPC & AI environments. 

We write these notes when we have something worth saying and update them 
as our thinking moves on. How we run an HPC/AI service is not a secret 
recipe, it's an approach which we can deliver to you or help you in 
using yourself.

The bottom line is that:

- **Control is a choice.** Managed doesn't mean that you have to hand over
  the keys to your service.
- **Health is not just hardware.** Scheduler throughput, user experience and
  service evolution are all parts of your service health. 
- **Process matters.** A repeatable approach to identifying, quantifying and
  addressing your service's health status is just as important as the 
  action taken
